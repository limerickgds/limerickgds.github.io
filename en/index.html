OUR RESEARCH STANDARD

Take a clear position.
Keep the evidence distinct.

Begin with primary sources. Separate what companies say, what the data establishes and what we infer. Every major judgment should provide a path to verification.

Explore the evidence ↗
01 / FACTS
Primary sourcesFinancial reports, papers and technical documentation. State the source, date and limits.
02 / CLAIMS
Company and team disclosuresKeep case studies and operating metrics attributed. They are not independent audits or universal industry results.
03 / INFERENCE
Judgment and scenario modelsMake assumptions and calculations explicit, including what evidence would change the conclusion.
04 / OPEN QUESTIONS
What we do not know yetDo not fill missing data with a story. Turn uncertainty into the next question to investigate.
OPEN RESEARCH

Read freely.
Check the reasoning.

Full articles, source ledgers and interactive models are open. Explore the sources, replace the assumptions and test whether the conclusions hold.
